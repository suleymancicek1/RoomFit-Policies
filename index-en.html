RoomFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

RoomFit ("we", "the app") is an AI-powered room layout application. This privacy policy explains how your personal data is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the RoomFit application ("Service").

By using our app, you agree to this privacy policy.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

To use the app, you need to create an account. We collect:

  • Email address (required)
  • Name (optional)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Google account information (if you sign in with Google)

2.2 Room and Furniture Data

For the app's core functionality, we collect:

  • Room measurements (width, length, ceiling height)
  • Fixed elements (doors, windows, radiators, AC units, columns)
  • Furniture details (type, dimensions, names)
  • Room photos (optional)
  • Furniture photos (optional)
  • Saved designs (when you save AI suggestions)

2.3 Usage Data

  • App usage statistics (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Error reports
  • Performance data

2.4 Location Data

❌ We do NOT collect location data. The app does not request or use location information.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide AI-powered room layout suggestions
  • Save and sync your designs
  • Improve app performance
  • Detect and fix issues
  • Communicate with you (support, updates)

4. Data Sharing

4.1 AI Services

Your room and furniture photos are sent to Anthropic (Claude API) for processing to generate AI suggestions. These photos are used solely for suggestion generation and are not stored by Anthropic.

4.2 Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication: Account verification
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Error tracking
  • Google Cloud: Data storage and backend

4.3 We Do NOT Share

❌ We do not share your data with advertisers, market researchers, or other third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention period: While your account is active
  • Deletion right: You can delete all your data via the "Delete Account" feature in the app
  • Automatic deletion: Inactive accounts are automatically deleted after 2 years

Account Deletion

In the app: Profile → My Account → Delete Account

6. Data Security

  • HTTPS encrypted communication
  • Firebase security rules
  • Regular security scans
  • Limited access control

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Your Rights (GDPR/KVKK)

You have the following rights:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to rectification and update
  • Right to erasure and deletion
  • Right to object to processing
