Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

RoomFit is an AI-powered room design and layout application. This privacy policy explains how your personal data is collected, processed, and protected when you use our application.

By using our app, you agree to this privacy policy.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (required) - for account verification and communication
  • Name (optional) - for profile personalization
  • Profile photo (optional) - for profile personalization
  • Google account information - when you sign in with Google

2.2 Room and Design Data

For the app's core functionality, we collect:

  • Room measurements - width, length, ceiling height
  • Fixed elements - doors, windows, radiators, AC units, columns
  • Furniture details - type, dimensions, names
  • Room photos (optional) - for AI suggestions
  • Furniture photos (optional) - for AI suggestions
  • Saved designs - when you save AI recommendations

2.3 Technical Data

  • Device information - model, OS version
  • Usage statistics - to improve app performance
  • Error reports - to detect and fix issues

⚠️ Data We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Location data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Personal financial information
  • Social media account data (except Google sign-in)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide AI-powered room layout suggestions
  • Save and synchronize your designs
  • Improve application performance
  • Detect and fix issues
  • Communicate with you (support, updates)

4. Data Sharing

4.1 AI Services

Your room and furniture photos are processed through a third-party AI API to generate design suggestions. These photos are used solely for suggestion generation and are not permanently stored by the API provider.

Note: The AI API provider may change. Check this page for updates.

4.2 Service Providers

  • Firebase Authentication: Account verification and security
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Error tracking and app stability
  • Google Cloud: Data storage and backend services

4.3 Data We Do NOT Share

❌ We do NOT share your data with:

  • Advertisers
  • Data brokers
  • Market research companies
  • Other third parties (except as required by law)

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention period: While your account is active
  • Deletion right: Delete all your data via the in-app "Delete Account" feature or this page
  • Automatic deletion: Inactive accounts are deleted after 2 years

6. Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS/TLS encrypted data transmission
  • Firebase security rules and access controls
  • Regular security scans and updates
  • Limited personnel access

7. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for a general audience. Parental supervision is recommended for users under 16. Parents or guardians should supervise their children's use of the app.

If we discover that a child under 16 has had their data processed, we will delete it immediately.

  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object
  • Right to object to automated decision-making
