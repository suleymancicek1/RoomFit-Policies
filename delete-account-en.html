Delete Your Account

Last updated: November 23, 2026

You can use this page to completely delete your RoomFit account. When your account is deleted, all your data will be permanently removed.

⚠️ Important Warning

Account deletion is irreversible. The following data will be permanently deleted:

  • All your homes and rooms
  • Saved designs and plans
  • Your uploaded photos
  • Profile information
  • AI suggestion history

Account Deletion Steps

  1. Open the app: Launch RoomFit on your phone.
  2. Go to Profile: Tap the "Profile" tab in the bottom right corner.
  3. Select My Account: Tap "My Account" from the profile menu.
  4. Click Delete Account: Press the "Delete Account" button at the bottom of the page.
  5. Confirm: Read the warning message and confirm the deletion.

After account deletion is complete, your Firebase ID and all application data will be permanently deleted. This process usually takes a few seconds.
